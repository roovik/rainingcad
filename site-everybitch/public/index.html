Introduction
Meet the Bitch

A poodle arrives ten minutes late and the whole block adjusts. That is the entire book in one cafe.

Chapter One
Scent of a Bitch

Two bitches meet on a sidewalk and swap everything that matters without a single name. You are still handing out business cards.

Chapter Two
Every Bitch Needs a Job

The Border Collie herds. The sheep are not consulted. A field guide to the she-dogs who run things and the bastards who get fired for it.

Chapter Three
Bitches Off the Leash

Top down. No destination. She is not running from anything, she is running toward the next thing, and she packed light.

Chapter Four
The Underbitch

The Chihuahua runs the boardroom but cannot be seen above the table. The Great Dane has noticed. Too late.

Chapter Five
The Bitch on the Line

Nobody shouted. Everybody got the message. Control, it turns out, is just power with a leash.

Chapter Six
The House Bitch

Three things to do today. Twelve things appeared. The coffee went cold and civilization survived anyway.

Chapter Seven
Big Bitch Energy

You know it when she walks in. The Great Dane in front of a pop art hot dog knows it too.

Chapter Eight
Season of the Bitch

The whole neighborhood knows before she does. Nobody says a word. Everybody takes notes.

Chapter Nine
Beware of the Bitch

The Doberman decides who gets past the velvet rope. There is no appeals process.

Chapter Ten
The Lap Bitch

She aims to occupy the best seat for every occasion. She will not explain the cushion.

Chapter Eleven
Bitching Up the Wrong Tree

Some trees look promising. Some squirrels drop nuts on your head. She keeps barking until she finds the right one.

Chapter Twelve
Son of a Bitch

Puppies are watching. The lessons that stick are rarely the ones she planned. A short history of who actually raised the bastard.

Chapter Thirteen
Lucky Bitch

She had excellent timing. People called it luck. She encouraged the misunderstanding.

Chapter Fourteen
Desperate Bitches

The neighbor bought a boat. She noticed. A field guide to keeping up, and the exact moment it stops being worth it.

Chapter Fifteen
The Alpha Bitch

The loudest bitch barks. The alpha listens. Then she walks the line, slowly, and everyone falls in.

Chapter Sixteen
Every Bitch Has Her Day

Nobody handed her a crown. Life rarely cooperates. The big day arrives disguised as an ordinary Tuesday.

Conclusion
The Tale Wagging the Bitch

The picnic winds down. The wine goes down. The squirrel gets away, and so does the afternoon. She keeps going.

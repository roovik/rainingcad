3Your notes

The notes you write on the day pages show up here too. Change them here and they print on your journal.

Handwritten your day?Tap Scan my handwriting under any day. Photograph or upload your page and we clean up the paper so only your pen shows. It prints in your own handwriting. A few lines per day fits best. For typed text, use Live Text on iPhone or Google Lens on Android, then paste it into the box.

Notes are saved on this device only. Use a backup file to move them to another device.

5Paper and print

Choose a paper size, then print or save a PDF.

For the best resultIn the print window, choose “Save as PDF” to keep a copy. Set scale to 100%, margins to None or Default, and turn off headers and footers. Print double sided if you like. On a phone, use Share, then Print, or save the PDF to Files.

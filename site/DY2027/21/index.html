Poodle

A genius in fancy dress, and it knows it.

The Poodle is German, whatever the accent suggests. It was bred along the rivers of Germany to retrieve shot ducks from cold water. The name admits it. Pudel comes from an old word for splashing about.

Even the absurd show clip began as work kit. Puffs of coat stayed over the joints and chest to keep them warm in the water. The rest was shaved to cut the drag.

France took one look and claimed it forever. The Poodle became the darling of the circus and the court. The world still files it as French by mistake.

Under the topiary is an athlete. One breed comes in three sizes, Standard, Miniature, and Toy, sorted by height alone.

It is also, by most accounts, the second cleverest dog alive, behind only the Border Collie. A Poodle learns fast, forgets nothing, and bores quickly. Leave one idle and it will appoint itself a project.

For all the dignity, the Poodle is a clown on purpose. Springy, theatrical, and stuck to its people like a shadow. You do not train a Poodle so much as negotiate with it.

Origin of the Name

The name is German, not French. Pudel comes from pudeln, to splash in water, after the breed’s first job retrieving ducks.

Notable Poodles

Sage, a Miniature Poodle, won Best in Show at Westminster in 2024. The breed has won the title many times.

Good to Know

The show clip was once practical. Puffs of coat kept joints warm in the water. Behind the grooming is a working water dog.

Coats, Colors & Sizes

A dense, curly, low-shedding coat in many colors. One breed in three sizes, Standard, Miniature, and Toy, by height alone.

By the Numbers

Often ranked second most intelligent, behind the Border Collie. Recognized by the AKC in 1887.

Cat Breeds

A Ranking and Character Study of the World's Most Beloved Felines

Fifty breeds, one countdown. From the gentle giants of the show ring to a werewolf-coated cat born in a Tennessee barn, this is a ranked tour of the most beloved felines on earth, counting down from fifty to the single cat at the very top. Every breed earns a full-page portrait and a page of what truly matters: where it came from, how it behaves, and what living with one is really like. Part field guide, part character study, part friendly argument. Includes an introduction and a contents page listing every breed by rank.

Richard J. Koret · 108 pages · 8 x 10 in · Full color

Dog Breeds

A Ranking and Character Study of America's Most Beloved Canines

Fifty breeds, one countdown. From the steady retrievers of the duck blind to a pocket-sized dog convinced it runs the household, this is a ranked tour of America's most beloved dogs, counting down from fifty to the single dog at the very top. Every breed earns a full-page portrait and a page of what truly matters: where it came from, how it behaves, and what living with one is really like. Part field guide, part character study, part friendly argument. Includes a contents page by rank and an alphabetical index of breeds.

Richard J. Koret · 110 pages · 8 x 10 in · Full color
